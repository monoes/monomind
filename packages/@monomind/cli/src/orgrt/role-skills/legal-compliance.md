# Legal Compliance — Best Practices

## Focus
Builds and maintains the organization's compliance posture — identifies applicable regulations, sets policy, monitors adherence, and ensures issues are caught and corrected before they become violations or liabilities.

## Best practices
- Start by mapping every regulation, standard, and internal policy actually applicable to the organization — don't assume coverage from a generic checklist.
- Translate regulatory requirements into concrete, auditable policies and procedures, not aspirational statements.
- Monitor for regulatory changes continuously; compliance programs reviewed only annually fall behind fast-moving requirements.
- Build in regular internal audits that gather documentation (policies, training records, performance metrics) proactively, before an external regulator asks.
- Maintain confidential, low-friction reporting channels for potential violations, and ensure findings actually reach a decision-maker.
- Apply consistent, documented enforcement — inconsistent discipline undermines the program's credibility and legal defensibility.
- Communicate compliance issues and required corrective actions clearly to affected teams, not just to leadership.

## Common pitfalls
- Writing policies that restate the regulation without specifying who does what, when.
- Treating compliance as a point-in-time audit rather than continuous monitoring.
- Failing to close the loop: identifying a gap but not tracking it through to remediation.
- Applying different scrutiny to different teams/individuals for the same violation type.

## Tools & techniques
- Regulatory inventory: a maintained list of every applicable law/standard with owner and review cadence.
- Gap-to-remediation tracker: each identified gap logged with root cause, fix, owner, and verification date.
- Control-to-requirement mapping: each policy/control explicitly linked to the regulatory requirement it satisfies (useful for audit defense).
- Periodic self-audit cadence: scheduled internal reviews that mirror what an external regulator would check.
